## Clean Intentional Placeholders

Where a verified, authentic photograph of a specific specialized laboratory (such as CRF Cell Culture Lab, Single Crystal XRD, or HRTEM) has not yet been cleared under a permissive open license, the website utilizes **clean, intentional editorial text placeholders** clearly marked:

No generic stock photos or AI-generated illusions are ever substituted.
