⚓ Captain's Advisory: Official National Gateways

All admissions are conducted strictly through sovereign national examination councils: JoSAA (JEE Advanced), COAP (GATE), JOAPS (JAM), and CAT. There are no unofficial seat bookings or back-alley shortcuts on our ship!

The Legendary Entrance Trials

Verified qualifications and official application portals for every level of scholar.

Undergraduate Engineering Trial

Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) & Dual Degree

Examination: JEE (Advanced)

Admission to all 4-Year B.Tech disciplines (Computer Science, Mining, Petroleum, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, Civil, Electronics, Metallurgy, Environmental) and 5-Year Integrated M.Tech.

Eligibility: Qualify JEE (Main), achieve qualifying rank in JEE (Advanced) conducted by the IIT Joint Admission Board (JAB), and participate in JoSAA seat allocation.

Postgraduate Engineering Trial

Master of Technology (M.Tech)

Examination: GATE

2-Year advanced research degree across specialized engineering fields, autonomous systems, power grids, and mineral processing.

Eligibility: Valid GATE score + participation in the Common Offer Acceptance Portal (COAP) and IIT (ISM) postgraduate portal.
☠ Enlist for M.Tech Fleet → ⚓ Division Curricula
Management Command

MBA & Business Analytics

Examination: CAT

Offered by the Department of Management Studies & Industrial Engineering. Ranked among NIRF's top management faculties in India.

Eligibility: Graduate degree (min 60% / 6.0 CGPA) + valid CAT percentile, followed by interview and profile screening.
