⚓ How to Navigate Here

Sailing to IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

By Rail

Dhanbad Junction (DHN)

3.5 km from the main gates. Major Grand Chord hub for Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, and Shatabdi trains connecting Kolkata, New Delhi, and Varanasi.

By Air (Nearest)

Kazi Nazrul Islam Airport (RDP)

Located in Andal / Durgapur, 90 km away via NH-19 expressway (~2 hours by car). Daily flights to Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad.

By Air (State Capital)

Birsa Munda Airport (IXR)

Located in Ranchi, 140 km away (~3.5 hours by car or direct express trains). High-frequency flights across all major Indian cities.
