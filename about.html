⚓ Origin Story

The Royal School of Mines Blueprint

In 1920, the McPherson Committee was commissioned to create India’s ultimate citadel for mining and earth sciences. They modeled it directly on London's Royal School of Mines and anchored in Dhanbad, the heart of India's mineral treasury.

On 9 December 1926, Lord Irwin inaugurated the Indian School of Mines. Then, in August 2016, the Parliament of India transformed it into an Indian Institute of Technology!

⚓ The Voyage Milestones

Captain's Log: Key Dates (1920–2026)

Historically verified milestones from parliamentary gazettes and centenary archives.

Year 1920 McPherson Committee Report

The Grand Expedition Blueprint

The Government of India orders the founding of a centralized mining and geological academy in Dhanbad, modeled after the Royal School of Mines in London.

Year 1926 Viceroy Lord Irwin Landfall

Inauguration of the Indian School of Mines

On 9 December 1926, the majestic red-brick Heritage Building and Diamond Hostel open their doors to India’s first cohorts of mining and geology pioneers.

Year 1957 Petroleum Expansion

Pioneering Petroleum Engineering in India

ISM pioneers India's first dedicated Petroleum Engineering and Applied Geophysics curricula, powering the discovery of India's earliest offshore and onshore oil reserves.

Year 2016 Parliament of India Act

Elevation to Indian Institute of Technology

Presidential assent given on 10 August 2016 to the Institutes of Technology (Amendment) Act, officially rechristening the institution as IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.

Year 2026 Centenary Milestone

100 Years of Exploration & Discovery

Celebrating 100 years of historic service with the launch of the new Centenary Academic Building and expanded Central Research Facility clusters!
