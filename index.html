⚓ Chapter 1: The First Landfall

The Royal School of Mines Legacy & The 1926 Foundation

Luffy explains: “Back in 1920, the McPherson Committee decided India needed the toughest navigators and miners in the world! They drew up blueprints based on the Royal School of Mines in London and laid anchor in Dhanbad because of the rich mineral veins beneath the soil.”

On 9 December 1926, Lord Irwin, the Viceroy of India, formally opened these gates as the Indian School of Mines. In 2016, following an Act of Parliament, the institution was elevated to an Indian Institute of Technology, uniting historic earth-sciences mastery with frontier artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and clean tech!
